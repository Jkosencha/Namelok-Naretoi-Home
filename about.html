About Namelok

Standing up for the children others hid away.

Namelok Naretoi Home is a community based organisation in Narok, Kenya. We began by finding children with disabilities who were hidden away in Maasai communities, and making sure they received medical care, schooling and a safe place to live.

Today we also protect girls rescued from FGM and early marriage, care for children from vulnerable homes, and run awareness programmes in schools and communities.

Our History

It started with one woman going house by house.

  1. 01
    1991 · Where it began

    The Search

    Our founder, Franciska, and a small team refused to accept that children with disabilities should be hidden away. In Maasai communities around Narok, many of these children were ignored, stigmatised and denied even the right to live. Franciska and her team went from house to house to find them, and when parents resisted, they worked through chiefs and community leaders to reach them.

  2. 02
    Healing

    From Hospital to the Classroom

    Finding a child was only the beginning. We took children to hospital for surgery, then saw them through rehabilitation, follow-ups and medical care until they were strong enough to go to school, and then we enrolled them. Throughout, Namelok was their home, and for children who could not return to their families, it became home for good.

  3. 03
    Protection

    A Safe Place for Girls

    Over time, Namelok grew to protect girls rescued from FGM and early marriage, and children from vulnerable homes. Every child comes to us through the proper channels: chiefs, District Officers and District Commissioners, and the courts when parents insist on marrying off a young girl for money or putting her through FGM.

  4. 04
    Advocacy

    A Champion for the Community

    Along the way, Franciska became a champion for her community. Through workshops and forums she taught that children with disabilities matter as much as any other child, and that educating girls matters as much as educating boys. Namelok became where vulnerable children and parents turn for help. We look carefully at every case and support as many as our funding allows.

  5. 05
    Today

    From School to School

    Today our team visits school after school, talking with girls and boys about education, their rights and their bodies, and showing them that a bright future is possible. We hand out sanitary pads, panties and boxers, and now balls, socks and books too.

Our Programs

Namelok is dedicated to providing a safe space, motivation and love to children from marginalised families.

Access to Healthcare

We provide access to essential medical treatment, physiotherapy, and rehabilitation for children living with disabilities or who have suffered trauma.

Support Inclusive Education

Education is at the heart of transformation. Ensure disabled children and rescued girls receive access to special needs and mainstream education, helping them integrate into schools based on their abilities.

Rescued Girls

Girls rescued from harmful practices such as FGM and child marriage are given safety, counseling, and opportunities to rebuild their lives and dreams.

Provide a Safe and Nurturing Home

We provide a loving, stable environment where children in recovery receive the care and resources they need.

School Awareness Program

We go from school to school talking with girls and boys about the value of education, their rights, and their bodies: hygiene, periods, protection and abstinence. Above all, we show them that a bright future is possible.

Donations for Children

On our visits we give out sanitary pads, panties and boxers, and more recently balls, socks and books, so children have what they need to learn, play and grow.
