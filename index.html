Dedicated to the most vulnerable children in our community since 1991.

Namelok Naretoi is a registered community based organisation founded in Narok, Kenya. We support children living with disabilities through medical rehabilitation and scholarships, rescue girls from FGM and early marriage, and provide shelter, counseling, and education to those who need it most.

Making a Difference

Four ways we care for the most vulnerable children in our community.

Healthcare

Medical treatment, physiotherapy and rehabilitation for children living with disabilities.

Inclusive Education

Scholarships and support in special needs and mainstream schools, based on each child's abilities.

Rescued Girls

Safety, counseling and a fresh start for girls escaping FGM and early marriage.

A Safe Home

A loving, stable place where children in recovery get the care and resources they need.

What Drives Us

The values and vision behind every decision we make.

Our Mission

Empowering children and families through love and action.

Fostering a community where disabled children and rescued girls are given the opportunity to thrive and contribute, breaking barriers and promoting inclusivity and equality.

Our Vision

A society where every child is seen, valued and supported.

Empowering disabled children and marginalized girls by providing comprehensive support for their successful integration and fulfillment into society as valued individuals.
